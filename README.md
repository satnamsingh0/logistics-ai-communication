# 🚚 Project: AI-Assisted Logistics Customer Service

### 📌 Business Problem

Communicating delayed shipment information accurately and clearly to customers and the internal coordination team.

### 🛠️ Tools Used

ChatGPT, prompt engineering techniques, and structured output formatting.

### 🧠 Strategies Practised

* 🎯 **Zero-Shot Prompting** – Generating responses without examples.
* 📝 **Context and Constraints** – Providing relevant details and setting clear limits.
* 👔 **Role Prompting** – Assigning specific roles to improve response quality.
* 💡 **Few-Shot Prompting** – Using examples to guide the response style.
* 📊 **Structured Output Prompting** – Organising information into tables and clear formats.
* 👥 **Audience-Based Prompting** – Adapting information for customers and internal teams.

### 📄 Outputs Created

* 📧 Customer email
* ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* 📋 Support information table
* 📱 Customer SMS
* 🏢 Internal coordination briefing

### 🙋 My Contribution

I experimented with different prompting techniques, improved prompts by adding context and constraints, checked the generated responses for accuracy and consistency, and corrected unclear or unsupported information. I also adapted the outputs for different audiences and formats.

### 🎓 Key Learning

I learned that clear instructions, relevant context, and specific constraints help generate more accurate and useful AI responses. I also understood the importance of verifying information, avoiding assumptions, and tailoring communication to the intended audience.

### ⚠️ Limitations

This project is a classroom simulation based on fictional shipment information. It is not connected to live shipment tracking systems, and the generated responses have not been used for actual customer communication.
